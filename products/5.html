Bucket bag

The Inès

A soft suede bucket that collapses flat when empty and holds its round shape once loaded — the drawstring does the structural work most bags ask a frame to do. The suede is treated for light rain, not submersion; we'd rather tell you the truth than oversell it. A short baton clasp keeps the drawstring from slipping loose on the train.
